## Goal

Two deliverables, no story written. FIRST, the decision: the
chronicle layer's six rulings, verbatim from the ops chat
(2026-07-27) — one living story per ITEM; the ending rule
(settled ⇒ written; later fixes incorporated, never a second
story); `docs/chronicle/` with `INDEX.md` shelving four shelves
(the away surface · the lane system · the information system ·
Roam); immutability beneath (record/ untouched, stories link DOWN
as footnotes); down-links only (a story never cites another
story's prose); supersession of the three-chapter shape queued in
[IDEAS](../../IDEAS.md) on 07-23. SECOND, the census:
`docs/chronicle/INDEX.md` born as a DRAFT — the proposed story
list per shelf, produced by the workshop's FIRST agent-team use
(three teammates, one shelf each, exploration only; the lead
carries the Roam shelf), each proposal carrying item · the
PRs/decides it spans · its current ending in one sentence ·
confidence. The census rides the gate report for the founder's
veto.

  is born on this bench.
- The ship-ritual synthesis-at-weld step — deferred by the
  founder's ruling to the first story weld (its own bench,
  touching ship.md).
- Inventing an ending the record cannot derive — the T3–T6
  batch-1 arc (four lanes launched 06-12, zero landed, no
  post-mortem on record) is marked "founder input owed", never
  filled.
- Teammates writing to the tree — they return proposals only.
