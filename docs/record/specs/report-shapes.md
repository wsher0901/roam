## Goal

Doctrine v2 split every response into two channels and made
process proof rather than prose. It did not say what a REPORT
contains. So each ritual grew its own shape, and the founder
reads six different layouts to answer the same three questions:
did it go well, what do I do, and where.

THE REPORT SKELETON fixes that: five slots in order — VERDICT ·
YOUR ACT · OFF-ORIGIN · one-breath context · a pointer to the
record. ONE JOB PER SURFACE, so the gate report carries ready +
the word, the external review carries decisions, and the shipped
report carries confirmation + needs-you + next. And PUSH FIRST,
REPORT SECOND: beyond the founder's acts and off-origin content, a
report may contain nothing not already on origin.

THE BOARD DIETS TO THE SAME PRINCIPLE — it is the standing report.
State and acts only; stories live in the record; position lives
once; Needs-you carries an urgency ladder; the baton renders STATE,
never doctrine.

## Out of scope

- **The masters.** No box master is touched.
- **Changing what any ritual DOES** — only what it says at its
  founder-facing turn-end, plus any writing step the standing duty
  proves is owed.
- **Rules 1–7 of the doctrine.** Rule 8 is appended; the rest are
  untouched.

## Deviations

1. **[COCKPIT-CHARTER](../../COCKPIT-CHARTER.md) went to v4 —
   against this spec's "no box master is touched".** The board's
   section rename left the charter's rule 1 telling an arriving
   cockpit to read its own seat from a heading that no longer
   exists: unfollowable at the very next flight. The charter's own
   law makes the consequence concrete — a master edit that never
   reaches the routine box fails SILENTLY. The bump re-opens the
   routine-box re-save the founder had just confirmed cleared, so
   it is back on the board's Needs-you and the full text prints in
   the shipped report. Surfaced at the gate as a deviation, not
   folded in quietly.

   [recall](../../skills/recall.md),
