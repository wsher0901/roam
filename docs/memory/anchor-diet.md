## What this task is

Decision headings became full statements, so their anchors became
full statements too — [D-067](../record/DECISIONS.md)'s runs 1,038
characters. THE SHORT-ANCHOR LAW cuts the heading to a short name
(8 words maximum) and moves the complete statement verbatim into
the entry body as `In full: …`. Anchors freeze at mint. D-001
through D-067 are retrofitted in this PR — a content-preserving
format change, sanctioned by the retroactivity law: append-only
protects the words, and every word survives one line lower.
