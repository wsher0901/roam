### THE GAP — two v5 sections the v6 text does not carry

The mandate ordered the fence replaced BYTE-FAITHFUL under the
[#80](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/80) precedent: flag
any gap, never reconstruct. There is a gap, and it is two whole
sections. v5's fence carried ten `##` sections; the approved v6
text carries twelve, but they are not a superset — **v5's
`## Conversation & pacing` and `## Style` appear nowhere in v6**
and were NOT carried over.

What they said, so the founder can rule on it with the content in
front of them rather than from memory:

- **Conversation & pacing** — one chat per topic, with a recap
  and a proposed title when discussion drifts into a distinct
  workstream; chat naming (roadmap IDs, or "Ops —" plus the
  topic); never
  encourage work ahead of its [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) stage;
  finish-first, with recommendations needing the founder's
  attention CAPPED AT TWO; and folding any roadmap-level change or
  new idea into the delivered prompt.
- **Style** — direct, concise, plain language for a solo builder
  without a CS background, "but never bare: keep the load-bearing
  reasoning"; trade-offs as 2–3 options with pros/cons and a
  recommendation; lead with the answer; explain purpose-shaped,
  last.

PARTLY ABSORBED, so the loss is smaller than two whole sections
suggests: doctrine v2's rule 4 (human-readable, plain words, "if
it looks stressful to read, it already failed") covers most of
Style's register, and v6's Prompt delivery keeps "Ideas or defects
voiced here ride the next delivered prompt as IDEAS lines. Never
leave a decision only in chat."

SURVIVING VIA LAWS, which v6 tells the surface to fetch when
unsure — two rules with the IDENTICAL survival path, so they get
the identical verdict (the critic caught the bench grading them

recommendation rule, in
[LAWS §Who you're working with](../LAWS.md#who-youre-working-with),
and the finish-first cap on what needs the founder's attention, in
the [pacing law](../LAWS.md#workflow-non-negotiable). Both are
reachable but no longer STATED on the Web surface, and a rule the

NOT ABSORBED ANYWHERE — independently confirmed by the critic,
