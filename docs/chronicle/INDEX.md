written row's ending lives in ITS STORY, which is the single place a
later weld rewrites; the ending column here is a summary and may lag
by design. This index lists the
PROPOSED story per item, produced by the [D-059](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-059--the-chronicle-layer) bench's census
(three read-only teammates, one shelf each, plus the lead on the
Roam shelf) and riding that bench's gate report for the founder's
veto. The governing rules live in ONE home — the
[D-059](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-059--the-chronicle-layer)
entry, pointed at rather than copied — and this shelf restates
only what its own reader operates by: ONE STORY PER ITEM, and the
ending rule — an item settled in any format gets its story
written, and a later fix is INCORPORATED into the existing story
with the ending rewritten at that weld, never a second story. An
ending the record cannot derive reads
"founder input owed", never a guess. Span cells name the PRs and
decides that advanced the item; per-item source files are in the
census capture
([the bench memory](../record/history/workshop/definition/chronicle-layer.md)).
