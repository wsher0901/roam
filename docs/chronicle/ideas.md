# IDEAS — the single inbox

> **CURRENT ENDING (2026-08-06).** The one and only inbox — no
> GitHub Issues, no boards — governed since
> [D-066](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-066--the-ideas-entry-contract)
> by an ENTRY CONTRACT stated at the file's own head: one idea per
> line, glyph first, two sections, plain language a tired reader
> scans. **The census cell is contradicted rather than merely
> stale**: it says closed entries are DELETED at harvest, which was
> [D-050](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-050--session-lifecycle-closed-is-not-dead)'s
> rule and has since been amended — closed entries now COMPRESS AND
> STAY, one line each, as the outcome ledger. Read at this writing
> the file carries **82 open and 21 closed** entries.

## What it is

The single door through which anything unplanned enters this
workshop. The moment the founder voices an idea or a defect
outside the current task's scope, one DATED line is appended here
unasked — and nothing here is scope until it is triaged into
[ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) via
[decide](../skills/decide.md).

THE ENTRY CONTRACT, which lives at the file's own head so its
reader cannot miss it:

- **One idea per line, one line per idea** — a plain sentence,
  jargon translated, links short-text at the tail. A multi-part
  thought SPLITS into separate lines so closure can happen per
  idea.
- **Glyph first** — ⏳ open · 🟢 developed, with its vehicle
  linked · ⚪ declined, with a one-word why.
- **Two sections** — OPEN, newest first · CLOSED, one line each.
- **Findings and probe stories are NOT ideas.** They live in the
  record; a line here points at them.

## What raised it

Two different problems, answered by the same file at two different
times.

The first was capture. An idea voiced mid-task is lost unless
something writes it down immediately, and the founder should not
have to remember to ask — so the law makes the append UNASKED and
routes every unplanned thought to exactly one place. One inbox
also means one place to look, which is why the workshop has
neither Issues nor a board.

The second problem was the file itself. An inbox with no shape
decays into a place things go to be forgotten: multi-part
entries that can never be individually closed, findings pasted in
at length, and no way to see at a glance what is open. By
2026-07 the file had drifted far enough that a contract, and an
enforcer, were needed.

## What changed, in order

- **Born as a LAWS clause** — the only inbox, appended to unasked,
  and nothing is scope until triaged through
  [decide](../skills/decide.md). The rule predates any contract
  about how a line should look.
- **[D-050](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-050--session-lifecycle-closed-is-not-dead)
  — IDEAS is an inbox, not an archive.** Closed entries were
  DELETED at every harvest, with the closing artifact named. The
  principle was right — an inbox that keeps everything stops being
  an inbox — and the mechanism turned out to be too blunt.
- **2026-07 —
  [D-066](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-066--the-ideas-entry-contract),
  the entry contract**
  ([#248](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/248)). The shape
  above becomes law, stated in the file's own header.
- **The amendment inside it, and the reason it matters.**
  D-050's delete-at-harvest half is REPEALED: closed entries
  COMPRESS AND STAY, one line each, as THE OUTCOME LEDGER. The
  inbox-not-archive principle survives in the COMPRESSION rather
  than in the deletion — a closed idea keeps its answer and its
  closing vehicle, and loses only its narrative. Deleting a closed
  idea destroyed the one record that it had ever been considered,
  so the same question could be raised, researched and declined
  repeatedly with nothing to show it had happened before.
- **The enforcement, because a shape with no enforcer decays back
  in one sitting.** The header IS the law;
  [handoff](../skills/handoff.md)'s harvest grooms the file to it
  EVERY SITTING — compressing, never deleting;
  [liftoff](../skills/liftoff.md),
  [decide](../skills/decide.md) and
  [parallel-lanes](../skills/parallel-lanes.md) cite it at their
  write steps; and both the ship-time critic and the external
  review grade IDEAS diffs against it.

## Where it stands

Live and under contract. Read at this writing the file carries
**82 open entries and 21 closed** — counts computed from the file
at write time under the derivation law, not copied from any
summary, and expected to move.

**THE CENSUS CELL IS WRONG, AND THIS STORY SAYS SO IN WORDS.** It
reads "The one inbox, self-pruning by rule: closed entries deleted
with their closing artifact named." That was true under
[D-050](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-050--session-lifecycle-closed-is-not-dead)
and stopped being true at
[D-066](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-066--the-ideas-entry-contract),
which the census predates. Closed entries are not deleted; they
compress and stay. The live file's header already states the
current rule, which is the reason the contract was put THERE
rather than only in a decision entry — a reader who opens IDEAS
meets the law before the content.

One structural note a future reader will want: a lane never writes
this file. Cloud and local lanes route what they surface into
their own memory under *Ideas surfaced*, and it reaches IDEAS only
through a ritual at the weld — which is why an idea found in
flight can lag the flight that found it.
