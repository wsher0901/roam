# recall — the read mirror

> **CURRENT ENDING (2026-08-06).** Shipped and standing, and the
> only item on this slice whose census cell still reads true when
> checked against the record. Founder questions about what this
> project has done, decided, or planned are answered by OPENING THE
> FILES and quoting them with anchors, never from what a session
> thinks it remembers — a read-only ritual that classifies the
> question into one of six routes, reads, and answers in plain
> sentences with a Sources block. It has gained one door since it
> shipped: the chronicle, so an answer reaches the item's LIVING
> story and not only the frozen record.

## What it is

The READ half of the routing law. The routing table at
[HOME §Where information goes](../HOME.md#where-information-goes)
says where every class of information gets WRITTEN;
[recall](../skills/recall.md) is the mirror that says where each
class gets READ BACK. It is model-invoked at Claude's own
discretion rather than typed as a command — it fires on any
founder question about past work, decisions, status, or
trajectory, whatever the phrasing, and on any moment an answer is
about to be produced from conversational memory when a file could
be opened instead.

Four steps, and the second is the whole point:

1. **Classify** — one question may hit several classes, and each
   part routes separately. Six classes, each with its doors in
   order: why/provenance · past work · ongoing status · future
   trajectory · term or mechanism · world fact or vocabulary.
2. **Look it up — read, don't recall.** Open the routed homes and
   take the answer from them. Counts and statuses are computed at
   answer time under the derivation law, never remembered.
3. **Answer in the founder's shape** — plain sentences, conclusion
   first, then one Sources block of deep links.
4. **The honesty rails** — the part that makes it trustworthy.

It never edits, commits, repaints, or merges. A question that
turns into a work request LEAVES recall and rejoins the normal
loop.

## What raised it

Conversations are disposable in this workshop, and a session that
answers from its own context is answering from the one place
nothing is stored. The failure it prevents is specific and worse
than forgetting: a plausible reconstruction is indistinguishable,
in tone, from a remembered fact. Both arrive fluent. Only one is
true, and the founder — working without a CS background and
relying on these answers to make calls — has no way to tell them
apart from the outside.

So the rule is not "try to remember accurately". It is that
remembering is not an allowed source.

## What changed, in order

- **[D-039](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-039--recall-answers-from-files)
  — recall is born as the routing table's read mirror**, shipped
  by [#123](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/123) on
  2026-07-14. Model-invoked at discretion rather than a typed
  command, because the moment it is needed most is the moment
  nobody thinks to ask for it.
- **The honesty rails are written as rails, not advice.** NOT
  FOUND is a real answer — "not recorded", plus where it WOULD
  live per the routing table, never a reconstruction from
  plausibility. Frozen wording in
  [DECISIONS](../record/DECISIONS.md) and
  [history/](../record/history) is quoted AS HISTORICAL even where
  a later decision superseded it, with the superseding D cited
  beside it. An ambiguous question gets the likeliest reading plus
  the names of the other doors checked.
- **The disposable-chat rail.** Recall retrieves what the RITUALS
  captured — memories, stories, D-entries,
  [IDEAS](../IDEAS.md). If something never left a conversation,
  the truthful answer is "not recorded", and the fix is routed to
  [handoff](../skills/handoff.md)'s harvest rather than papered
  over by recall. The skill names its own limit instead of
  disguising it.
- **git outranks the board, inside the answer.** For anything
  live, step 2 checks branches, PRs and checks directly, and says
  so plainly when [the board](../DASHBOARD.md) disagrees. The
  read mirror inherits the board's own posture rather than
  trusting the rendered surface.
- **2026-07 — THE CHRONICLE DOOR is added**
  ([D-070](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-070--baton-renderings-single-homed),
  [#257](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/257)). Two of the
  six classes — why/provenance and past work — now end at
  [the chronicle](../chronicle/INDEX.md) for the item's LIVING
  story, read alongside the frozen record. This is the one change
  that altered what recall can answer rather than how: before it,
  every door led to a record frozen at its ship date, and "what
  happened SINCE" had no home to route to.

## Where it stands

Live, and the least dramatic item on this slice — no defect, no
reversal, no open question. Its census cell reads "Shipped and
standing: founder questions answered from the files with receipts,
never from conversational memory", and the record confirms it
without amendment. **Six of this slice's seven cells did not
survive that check; this one did.**

One precision the cell omits rather than gets wrong: its span
names
[D-039](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-039--recall-answers-from-files)
and then two later PRs, skipping
[#123](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/123) — the PR that
actually shipped the skill.

What is NOT provable from the record is how often recall fires,
because a model-invoked read-only ritual leaves no artifact when
it works: no commit, no PR, no board line. Its successes are
invisible by construction and only its failures would ever be
visible. That is a real limit on this ending, and it is stated
rather than rounded up to "in daily use".
