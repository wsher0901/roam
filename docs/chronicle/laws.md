# LAWS — the always-loaded constitution

> **CURRENT ENDING (2026-08-06).** `docs/LAWS.md` is the workshop's
> constitution, and the only file guaranteed to be in front of every
> session: the root `CLAUDE.md` is a title line and the import
> `@docs/LAWS.md`. Since
> [D-069](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-069--the-law-register-and-the-manual-groom)
> it is a REGISTER rather than prose — every law renders NAME → the
> rule in one to three plain sentences → a pointer to its mechanics
> and its D-number — across eleven sections holding universals only,
> with procedures expelled to [skills](../skills) and rationale to
> [DECISIONS](../record/DECISIONS.md), [HOME](../HOME.md) and
> [ENGINE](../ENGINE.md). **The census cell for this item is STALE
> and its span stops one entry too early.** It ends at
> [D-053](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-053--guardrail-audit), the
> 2026-07-24 audit that found the harness already at best practice —
> true on its date and nothing later. Since that audit LAWS was
> recut to the register, minted three laws it had only been
> practising (verification · sweep · box-master), had the founder's
> touchpoints corrected from two to three, and **surrendered the one
> concrete thing D-053 had just put into it.**

## What it is

The file that governs by being unavoidable. Every other document in
this workshop has to be found; this one arrives. That single
property is what decides its content: LAWS carries universal laws
only — rules that bind in every session regardless of what the
session is doing — because anything conditional would be a tax paid
by every sitting that does not need it.

Its length is therefore a design constraint rather than an
accident, and the recurring question in its history is not "is this
rule true?" but "does this rule have to be HERE?"

Eleven sections at HEAD: what we're building · who you're working
with · the three touchpoints · workflow · task anatomy · parallel
lanes & cloud · knowledge & tracking · safety · self-improvement ·
tech stack · equipment.

## What raised it

The workflow kit ([#1](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/1),
2026-06-10) — the repository's first substantive commit. There was
no separate constitution at the start; there were working rules in
a root instruction file, and the whole subsequent history of this
item is those rules being separated from everything that merely
surrounded them.

## What changed, in order

- **[#1](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/1) — the workflow
  kit.** The rules exist, mixed in with everything else.
- **[#62](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/62) — rituals count
  for themselves.** The ritual layer starts pulling procedure out of
  the governing text; the ship-tail goes quiet.
- **[#71](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/71) /
  [D-024](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-024--architecture-v2-narrative-layer-and-skills)
  — architecture v2, and the moment LAWS becomes THE constitution.**
  The rituals become skills — thin stubs in `.claude/`, procedures
  vault-readable in `docs/skills/` — and root `CLAUDE.md` **reduces
  to a two-line import of LAWS**. This is the structural decision
  the rest of the story elaborates: from here, being always-loaded
  is LAWS' definition rather than one of its properties.
- **[D-027](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-027--laws-polish) — polish, and
  the readability argument.** LAWS drops its four inline
  D-citations: the weave rule stands for relationships, but
  provenance is the consolidations' job. The bench-first bullet and
  the seven-point lane law gain ONE-LINE WHYS, and the header names
  the three rationale homes. The stated reason is specific and
  recurs — mid-sentence citation anchors hurt the constitution's
  readability for a solo founder without a CS background, and laws
  that carry their why are followed better.
- **[D-029](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-029--corpus-writing-laws) /
  [#121](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/121) — the corpus
  laws arrive, and LAWS is granted an exception.** The links-below
  law binds every living file; the restyle sweep classes LAWS as
  COMMAND-CLASS, its functional pointer links kept inline because a
  command that makes you look elsewhere for its target is a worse
  command.
- **[D-037](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-037--pickup-speaks-the-founders-shape)
  — LAWS binds, HOME renders.** §Knowledge & tracking gains the
  one-home clause POINTING at HOME's routing table rather than
  reproducing it. The division of labour is explicit and becomes the
  house pattern: the binding clause is a law, the table is a manual.
- **[#134](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/134) — tightness,
  Option C.** Command plus one-line whys, with the procedure grain
  expelled to [handoff §1.5](../skills/handoff.md#15--park-the-local-lanes-full-only);
  the stale decide trigger and a wrong conditional fixed. LAWS stops
  explaining how and confines itself to what and whether.
- **[#147](https://github.com/wsher0901/roam/pull/147) — the time
  clause.** The derivation law grows its second half: time is
  derived, never recalled.
- **[D-053](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-053--guardrail-audit) — audited,
  and found already compliant.** The founder's maximum-depth read of
  the whole governing layer against Anthropic's guidance — the
  always-loaded file governs, verification loops multiply quality,
  sparse emphasis outperforms shouting — returned ALREADY AT BEST
  PRACTICE: marker density 1–7 per file, thin single-source stubs,
  mechanics living in hooks. Its two real improvements were small,
  and one of them was to promote the CI-mirror sentence into LAWS as
  a labeled, copy-runnable command block, on the reasoning that
  verification loops earn promotion by being run.
- **[D-069](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-069--the-law-register-and-the-manual-groom)
  — the register, three new laws, and the promotion undone.** LAWS
  is recut so every law renders NAME → rule → pointer. Three laws
  the workshop had only been PRACTISING are written down: THE
  VERIFICATION LAW (verify before drop, verify before classify), THE
  SWEEP LAW (grep the destination; the proof of a sweep is a re-run
  returning zero), and THE BOX-MASTER LINE. §The two touchpoints
  becomes §THE THREE TOUCHPOINTS — a correction, not an addition,
  since the review word had been law since
  [D-046](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-046--flight-cockpit-the-control-tower-online)
  while the file naming the founder's duties still said there were
  two. "Dispatch" gives way to "delegation" throughout. **And the
  verification command fence leaves LAWS for a pointer at
  [ship §1](../skills/ship.md#1--preflight)** — a second copy the
  routing law forbids, and one that had already begun to drift from
  what ship actually performs.
- **Growth since, none of it in the census.** The wake rule
  generalized
  ([D-062](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-062--the-wake-rule-generalized)),
  the box-master class
  ([D-064](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-064--the-box-master-class-and-setup-entry-contract)
  ·
  [D-065](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-065--the-box-master-diet)), and

- **A second line beside the import.** Root `CLAUDE.md` is no longer

## Where it stands

A register of universals, loaded everywhere, with its procedures
somewhere else. The audit that examined it found nothing wrong, and
the interesting fact about this item is what happened NEXT — because
the story of LAWS after its clean audit is more instructive than the
audit.

**D-053's one concrete improvement was reversed sixteen entries
later, by the constitution's own law.** The audit promoted the
verification loop INTO LAWS because a copy-runnable block gets run
more reliably than prose. D-069 removed it, because
the routing law that LAWS itself carries
([D-036](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-036--information-routing-one-home-per-class))
forbids a second copy — and because the copy had already started drifting from
ship, which is exactly the failure the one-home rule exists to
prevent. Both decisions were right on their own terms. What settles
between them is a rule about this file specifically: **a good idea
is not automatically a reason to put something in LAWS**, because
the cost of a second copy is paid in drift and the cost of length is
paid every sitting.

That is also why the census cell reads as it does. Stopping at
"audited as already at best practice" is not a wrong sentence; it is
a sentence that was true on 2026-07-24 and has been overtaken by the
recut, three minted laws, a corrected touchpoint count, and the
removal of the very block the audit added. A dated attestation is
evidence of its date and of nothing later.
