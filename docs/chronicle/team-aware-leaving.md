# Team-aware leaving — capture, then kill

> **CURRENT ENDING (2026-08-06).** Every ritual road out of a
> sitting captures a live agent team's findings BEFORE killing it,
> in that order, because the order is the decision. **The census
> cell's second clause is now FALSE and this is the correction.** It
> ends "the superseded-seat self-close stays uncovered and filed" —
> true when written on 2026-07-28, and closed IN FULL since by
> [D-062](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-062--the-wake-rule-generalized),
> which makes ANY superseded seat exit by its own closing ritual: a
> cockpit by [land](land.md), a ground control tower by
> capture-then-close **with team findings captured first**. The rule
> is not merely decided but LIVE in
> [LAWS §Parallel lanes & cloud](../LAWS.md#parallel-lanes--cloud),
> which carries it in those words. The one road out that ran no
> ritual — the single place a team's findings could die silently —
> no longer exists.

## What it is

A duty attached to every exit from a sitting: if an agent team is
live, its findings go into the record BEFORE a single shutdown
request, and only then is the team shut down by name and each
result reported.

The prohibition underneath it is that **a live team is never
PARKED.** Parking is a lane act, and a lane survives on its branch —
which is what a stamped Status holds. A team has no branch and no
Status, so a parked team is not a thing that can exist.

## What raised it

[D-055](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-055--agent-teams-the-boundary) made
single-sitting-only a law and left it **enforced by nothing.** The
mechanism is unambiguous: `/resume` and `/rewind` do not restore
in-process teammates, so a resumed lead may go on messaging
teammates that no longer exist. But a rule documented on two
surfaces and acted on by NO RITUAL is a rule that will be broken the
first time a team happens to be running when the founder says "done
for today".

## What changed, in order

- **[D-055](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-055--agent-teams-the-boundary)
  — the boundary, and two risks that became law.** Teams are for
  EXPLORATION AND REVIEW at a ground seat; **lanes remain the
  authorship mechanism.** The reasons are stated rather than
  asserted: lanes are cloud-isolated, PR-gated, canary-claimed and
  wake-locked with days of hardening behind them, while teams are
  local, in-session, share ONE working tree, and have no PR
  isolation. Risk A is single-sitting-only — an explicit carve-out
  to the interrupt promise, and the sentence that carries it is
  worth keeping whole: **an interrupt is safe for your session and
  fatal to your teammates.** Risk B is that a TEAMMATE-AUTHORED DIFF
  IS SELF-AUTHORED — a teammate is a full session pushing as the
  founder, so it earns no independent review merely by being a
  second agent.
- **[D-056](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-056--team-aware-leaving-rituals)
  — the rituals learn about teams, and the DUTY INVERTS.** The
  candidate line that opened the bench said "a live team is finished
  or shut down, never parked" — and it stopped at the PROCESSES.
  **The loss it prevents is not the one that wording suggests.**
  Teammates are cheap: they die cheaply and respawn cheaply. What
  cannot be re-derived is what the team FOUND, because a team's
  output lives in transcripts that vanish with the session while git
  keeps everything else. So the duty inverts to CAPTURE, THEN KILL —
  and that inversion is the decision; the prohibition survives
  inside it.
- **Two honesty clauses ride with it.** Shutdown is NOT INSTANT — a
  teammate finishes its current request or tool call first, so the
  step waits rather than assumes. And a teammate that REFUSES OR
  HANGS is named plainly in the close report on the road out, never
  smoothed into a clean exit. Auto-shutdown without reporting was
  rejected in those terms: **a silent teardown is fast and loses
  exactly the thing worth keeping — a ritual that swallows the
  findings is worse than one that does nothing, because it looks
  clean.**
- **Land carries NO team procedure at all**, and the basis is stated
  as what it is: POLICY, not a platform limit. The platform would
  let any session spawn teammates; the boundary is what says a
  cockpit does not. Resting the rule on one-team-per-session instead
  would have founded it on a mechanism that does not imply it and
  could change. So land holds one routing line to handoff, and
  duplicating the procedure was rejected — **a copy there would be a
  procedure that can never run, drifting quietly out of step with
  the one that can.**
- **The ⏸ block gains one conditional field**, TEAM FINDINGS,
  written only when a team was live at the pause: **the one field
  git cannot re-derive.** Omitted entirely when no team was running.
- **[D-062](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-062--the-wake-rule-generalized)
  — the last road is closed.** D-056 covered every exit that RUNS A
  RITUAL — handoff FULL and QUIET, liftoff, land, go-remote, Esc —
  and supersession was the one road out that ran none: "push, write
  nothing". That made it the single place a team's findings could
  die silently, since nothing was LICENSED to write them down. The
  generalization costs nothing new: **every seat already HAS a
  closing ritual, and the rule is simply that supersession routes to
  it rather than around it.**

## Where it stands

**Closed, and closed in the shape the record can be checked
against.** The law names both cases — cockpit by land, ground tower
by capture-then-close, team findings first — and the two decisions
behind it are on the books. The census cell was accurate on the day
it was written and was overtaken four days later; nothing about it
was careless, which is exactly the argument the ending rule makes.

**The no-board clause is what made the generalization safe.** The
original reason supersession said "write nothing" was to stop a dead
seat fighting a live one over shared surfaces — a real hazard, and
one D-061's bench had already found live: a superseded cockpit's
MODE R repaint would have erased the takeover stamp of the desk that
superseded it. So the hazard survives intact, narrowed to what
actually causes it. A superseded seat now writes its OWN homes — its
bench memory, its lanes' fences — and nothing belonging to the seat
that now holds the baton.

**What remains unenforced is the same class of thing as everywhere
else on this shelf: the honesty clauses.** Nothing mechanically
checks that a hung teammate is named, or that the shutdown step
actually waited rather than assumed. They are prose promises, and
the workshop knows what prose promises are worth — which is why the
ORDER (capture first) was made structural, written into the step's
position rather than into an instruction to be careful.
