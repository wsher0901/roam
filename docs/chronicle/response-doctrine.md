# The response doctrine — one standard for every surface that speaks to the founder

> **CURRENT ENDING (2026-08-06).** How every surface speaks to the
> founder is ONE standard with ONE home,
> [HOME §Response doctrine](../HOME.md#response-doctrine); every
> ritual and charter REFERENCES it and none copies it. **The census
> cell is the most stale of this slice and understates the item by
> two full revisions.** It reads "settled: one home in HOME, every
> ritual referencing it and none copying it", citing
> [D-052](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-052--response-doctrine) alone —
> which was FIVE clauses. At HEAD the doctrine has EIGHT.
> [D-063](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-063--response-doctrine-v2-and-web-instructions-v6)
> took it to v2, seven rules led by a new first rule that
> reorganizes every other — TWO CHANNELS: sentences for the founder,
> fenced blocks for machines, and no technical content outside a
> fence. Then
> [D-067](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-067--the-report-skeleton-and-the-board-diet)
> added rule 8, THE REPORT SKELETON, whose second half is the
> sharpest instruction in the set: **PUSH FIRST, REPORT SECOND.**
> The cell's error is not a wrong fact but a wrong certification —
> it calls "settled" an item that has moved twice since.

## What it is

A standard for the workshop's output, in the same way the corpus
writing laws are a standard for its documents.

It exists because a solo founder without a CS background is the sole
consumer of everything every session produces, across at least five
surfaces — desk sessions, cloud lanes, the cockpit, the Web
instruction box, and the standing board. Left to itself, each
surface develops its own register, and the founder pays the
translation cost every time.

At HEAD it is eight rules. Two channels. Three questions in order
with timing — what is the situation, what is needed from the founder
(counted), what exactly does the founder do and WHEN. Process is
proof, not prose. Human-readable, with the memorable acceptance
test: *if it looks stressful to read, it already failed.* Depth on
request. State derived live. Glyph verdicts leading every status
item. And the report skeleton.

## What raised it

The founder's own statement of the goal, recorded verbatim in
substance: minimum reading time to decision, explicit steps over
abstractions, purpose-shaped explanation.

That phrasing is worth preserving because it is a specification, not
a complaint. "Minimum reading time to decision" names the quantity
being optimized, and most of the doctrine's later development is
that quantity being pursued into places the first version did not
reach.

## What changed, in order

- **[D-052](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-052--response-doctrine) — v1,
  five clauses, and the one-home discipline enforced on arrival.**
  Answer first · steps are explicit, never "give the word" but the
  word itself and where it goes, every prompt carrying WHERE ·
  WHAT · WHY · explanation purpose-shaped and LAST · state derived
  live · glyph verdicts, every 🔴 naming the fix AND where. Every
  ritual and charter REFERENCES the section; none copies it — and
  the entry records that this was VERIFIED BY NEGATIVE-GREP on one
  clause's own phrasing rather than asserted. The same decision set
  the cockpit's fleet-table report shape and rewrote three rituals'
  status templates to a four-part shape.
- **[D-063](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-063--response-doctrine-v2-and-web-instructions-v6)
  — v2, and a new rule that reorders the rest.** TWO CHANNELS leads:
  sentences for the founder, fenced blocks for machines, NO
  TECHNICAL CONTENT OUTSIDE A FENCE. It is placed first because it
  reorganizes every other rule — once the channel is decided, most
  register questions answer themselves. Alongside it: three
  questions gain TIMING (now · after ⟨event⟩ · nothing), and PROCESS
  IS PROOF, NOT PROSE arrives — verification detail lives in the
  block or the record, and the prose states only the outcome. The
  glyph set grows ⏸ and ⏳. The Web instruction master goes v5 → v6
  carrying the doctrine.
- **[D-067](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-067--the-report-skeleton-and-the-board-diet)
  — rule 8, and the discipline that makes it verifiable.** Every
  founder-facing work-report turn-end, on every surface, ritual or
  not, renders FIVE SLOTS IN ORDER: VERDICT (glyph + state + finding
  count) · YOUR ACT (the exact word or paste · which surface ·
  when — or the word "nothing", which is an answer) · OFF-ORIGIN
  (content existing only in this session; **omitted entirely when
  there is none, so its presence means something**) · one-breath
  context · a pointer to the record, never a retelling. And the
  clause the whole rule turns on: **PUSH FIRST, REPORT SECOND** —
  beyond the founder's acts and off-origin content, a report may
  contain nothing that is not already on origin. The same entry
  puts the DASHBOARD on the same diet, as the standing report.
- **The bill that rule 8 sent to a neighbouring item.** D-067's own
  heading ran to 1,038 characters and was cited five times — written
  by the very bench dieting every other surface — which is named in
  [D-068](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-068--the-short-anchor-law) as the
  reason the short-anchor law had to exist.
- **A second line beside the constitution's import.** Root
  `CLAUDE.md` now carries the report skeleton as a standing
  instruction of its own — the only doctrine clause promoted to the
  always-loaded file, and it POINTS at HOME rule 8 rather than
  restating the five slots.

## Where it stands

Eight rules, one home, referenced everywhere and copied nowhere.

The doctrine's real achievement is rule 8's second half, and it is
worth separating from the formatting. **PUSH FIRST, REPORT SECOND**
is not a style rule at all: it is a constraint on when a session may
speak, and it converts every report into a claim that can be checked
against origin. A report that may contain nothing not already pushed
cannot contain an unverifiable status claim, because there is
nowhere for one to live. That closes by construction the failure the
derivation law addresses by discipline — and it is the reason the
OFF-ORIGIN slot exists at all, as the single sanctioned exception,
made conspicuous by being omitted whenever it is empty.

**The census cell fails in an instructive way.** Every sentence in
it is still true — the doctrine does have one home, rituals do
reference rather than copy — so a reader checking it for errors
would find none. What is wrong is the word "settled" attached to a
citation of v1 only. An item that has been revised twice since its
cell was written is not settled; it is moving, and the cell's own
confidence rating of "high" makes it more likely to stop a reader
than a hedged one would. This is precisely the hazard the bench's
brief warned about: a dated attestation is evidence of its date and
of nothing later, and the reader who stops at a confident stale cell
never learns what the record would have told them.
