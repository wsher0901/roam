# HOME — the manual and encyclopedia

> **CURRENT ENDING (2026-08-06).** The living manual, and the
> named home for everything the other files deliberately refuse to
> explain: the routing table, the board's reading keys, the
> response doctrine, the command card, Terms, and — since
> [D-065](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-065--the-box-master-diet) — the
> cockpit's connector ladder. Its census cell lists those roles
> correctly and predates the change that made the arrangement a
> LAW: at
> [D-069](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-069--the-law-register-and-the-manual-groom)
> [LAWS](../LAWS.md) was recut into a REGISTER carrying rules
> only, which is what turned "HOME explains things" from a habit
> into the other half of a two-file contract. HOME was groomed in
> the same PR and stands at **thirteen sections**.

## What it is

The file a reader opens when they want to know WHY, or WHAT
something is called, or HOW a mechanism actually works. Thirteen
sections, counted at this writing: Start here · A day in the
workshop · The files — what each one is for · The mechanisms ·
Reading the board · Response doctrine · Command card · Agent teams
· Where information goes · Terms · Reading the data files ·
Equipment & environment · Roadmap manual.

It is defined as much by what it takes OFF other files as by what
it holds. The workshop's rule is that **files carry content, not
explanations** — so file purposes, mechanisms, notation and terms
live here, and every other file is free to be short.

Two of its sections are load-bearing far beyond the manual:

- **§Where information goes** — the routing table, the ONE
  authoritative statement of which class of information lives
  where, with its named writer. The routing law is enforced
  against this table.
- **§Response doctrine** — the single standard for every surface
  that speaks to the founder, cited by every ritual and copied by
  none.

## What raised it

A constitution that explains itself stops being readable as a
constitution. [LAWS](../LAWS.md) loads into EVERY session through
the root `CLAUDE.md` import, so its length is a tax paid on every
sitting, and its shape is what a session actually obeys. Left
alone it drifts toward prose — laws re-explaining mechanics that
live in a skill, a verification loop quoted in full where its one
home is elsewhere.

The pressure is real in both directions: a rule with no
explanation is followed badly, and an explanation inside the rule
is paid for by every future session. HOME is where that tension
gets resolved — the explanation exists, in full, exactly one hop
away.

  and after — HOME's files table becomes a maintained surface.**
  Its ATLAS row tracked that page from six diagrams to seven to

  retroactivity law visible in a single table cell.
- **2026-07 — THE CONNECTOR LADDER MOVES IN**
  ([D-065](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-065--the-box-master-diet),

  box-master diet ruled that procedures may not ride inside an
  external box, so the cockpit charter's embedded recovery ladder
  relocated to HOME §Mechanisms, joining the dependency map that
  already explained it. Relocation, not repeal — and a clean
  demonstration of what HOME is FOR: the charter keeps the
  standing duty (climb the ladder, never improvise) and HOME
  carries the ladder.
- **2026-07 — THE MANUAL GROOM**
  ([D-069](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-069--the-law-register-and-the-manual-groom),

  day sections merge into §A day in the workshop, §Terms gains six
  rows, and §Reading the board stops restating the board spec,
  reducing to the reading keys plus a pointer to
  [handoff §4](../skills/handoff.md#4--repaint-dashboard-the-board-spec--single-source).

## Where it stands

Live, groomed, and thirteen sections long — counted from the file
at write time.

**On the census cell.** It is the most accurate cell on this
slice after recall's: "The living manual: one hop from any
why-question, and the one home for the routing table, the board
legend, the response doctrine, the command card, and Terms." Every
role it names is still HOME's. What it cannot show, having been
written on 2026-07-27, is that the arrangement was formalized
three days later — before
[D-069](../record/DECISIONS.md#d-069--the-law-register-and-the-manual-groom)
the division of labour between LAWS and HOME was a practice, and
after it the register shape makes the split structural. The cell's
list is also now one item short: the connector ladder.

The honest limit on this ending is size. At roughly two thousand
lines, HOME is by far the largest living file in the workshop, and
"one hop from any why-question" is a claim about NAVIGABILITY that
nothing mechanically tests. The link checker proves its pointers
resolve; no check proves a reader can find the right section. The
groom improved that and did not settle it, which is why this
ending describes a maintained file rather than a finished one.
